\begin{abstract}
\noindent Small Italian hauliers spend around 30\% of their costs on diesel and cannot absorb monthly
jumps such as +32\% in March 2022. FuelGuard is a monthly signal, offered by a bank to its SME transport
clients, that predicts whether next month's diesel cost will exceed the price that can be locked today by more
than 5\%, and recommends how much of the consumption to fix. Using 21 daily market series from Bloomberg
and the ECB (2007--2026, 57 engineered features) and a purged expanding-window walk-forward over 15 test
years, we find that (i) the size of next month's premium is not predictable beyond the futures price; (ii)
spikes are predictable to a modest but significant degree (AUC up to 0.69, Brier skill +5\%); (iii) most of
this skill comes from implied volatility, which alone matches or beats random forests and gradient boosting;
and (iv) hedging when risk is high halves the cost of the worst 5\% of months (CVaR from 16.7\% to 6.8--9.5\%)
at a lower average cost than a fixed hedge. We discuss what the product cannot promise and why a parsimonious
model is the right one to sell.
\end{abstract}
