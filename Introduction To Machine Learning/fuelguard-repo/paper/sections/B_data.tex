\section{Data dictionary and descriptive statistics}\label{app:data}

Daily observations on NYMEX diesel trading days, January 2007 -- November 2026 (before the one-year
burn-in used by the features). Sources: Bloomberg (\texttt{BDH}, field \texttt{PX\_SETTLE} for futures,
\texttt{PX\_LAST} for indices, \texttt{30DAY\_IMPVOL\_100.0\%MNY\_DF} for implied volatilities) and the ECB
(euro reference rates). Diesel in EUR/litre is computed by the authors.

\paragraph{Features (57).} \emph{Momentum (33):} returns over 5, 21 and 63 days of diesel (EUR), Brent,
WTI (2nd month), natural gas, copper, gold, S\&P~500, S\&P~500 Energy, STOXX~600 Oil \& Gas, 10-year
Treasury futures and EUR/USD. \emph{Futures curve (4):} second/first-month slope for diesel, Brent, natural
gas and WTI. \emph{Refining margin (2):} diesel crack spread $0.42\,\mathrm{HO1}-\mathrm{CO1}$ (USD/bbl) and
its 21-day change. \emph{Volatility (8):} diesel and Brent implied volatility, diesel realised volatility
over 21 and 63 days, implied minus realised, 21-day change in implied volatility, VIX, VIX term structure
(VIX3M/VIX). \emph{Inflation expectations (4):} US 5-year breakeven and euro 5-year inflation swap, levels and
21-day changes. \emph{Price level (2):} diesel price relative to its 252-day mean, and its z-score.
\emph{Past outcomes (2):} premium realised by the last decision whose outcome is fully known (lag 23 days)
and the spike frequency over the previous year. \emph{Seasonality (2):} sine and cosine of the month.
